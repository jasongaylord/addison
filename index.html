More About Addi

I'm on track to graduate from Wyoming Area High School in 2027. I'll be entering my junior year of high school in August 2025 with a 3.8 grade point average. I have also been elected to the National Honor Society. In college, I hope to pursue a degree in accounting or finance.

I've been a starter for both softball and basketball since my freshman year. I was chosen as a first team all-star of the Wyoming Valley Conference in my freshmen year and a second team all-star this past year. Regardless of sport, you'll typically find me wearing #2.
