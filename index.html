More About Addi

I'm on track to graduate from Wyoming Area High School in 2027. I've entered my junior year of high school in August 2025 with a 3.8 grade point average. I have also been elected to the National Honor Society. In college, I plan to pursue a degree in accounting or finance.

I've been a starter for both softball and basketball since my freshman year. I was chosen as a first team all-star of the Wyoming Valley Conference in my freshmen year and a second team all-star this past year. While my strongest sports passion is softball, I have played basketball since 2nd grade and have been a runner on the cross country team since 7th grade. Regardless of sport, you'll typically find me wearing #2 and sometimes #11.

Outside of school and sports, I also enjoy spending time with my family and friends and working at a local ice cream shop. I have been involved in various community service projects, including volunteering for a 5K that my sister organizes to raise money for Children with Hunger.
